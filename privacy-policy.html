Bsun Sat-Nav

Privacy Policy

Developed by Bisan Subba

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Introduction

Bsun Sat-Nav is an independently developed, experimental browser-based GPS navigation application.

This policy explains how location information and other data are used when you access the application.

2. GPS and Location Information

Bsun Sat-Nav uses your browser's Geolocation API after you grant location permission and start GPS tracking.

Latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy, speed, heading and update time are used for location display, route monitoring and navigation guidance.

You can stop GPS tracking or withdraw location permission through your browser or device settings.

3. External Services

Bsun Sat-Nav uses the following services:

  • OpenFreeMap — map display and map tiles.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — destination searches.
  • OSRM — driving route calculations.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • UNPKG — delivery of MapLibre files.

Destination search terms are sent to Nominatim. Route requests send starting and destination coordinates to OSRM.

Map requests may reveal the approximate geographical area being viewed.

These providers may receive IP addresses and technical request information, and may process or retain it under their own privacy policies, including outside the United Kingdom.

4. Voice Guidance

Spoken navigation instructions use browser or device speech synthesis. Depending on your browser and operating system, speech processing may involve an external provider.

5. Data Storage and Retention

The current experimental application does not implement developer-managed accounts, a central journey-history database or application analytics.

Destination search results may be cached temporarily in browser memory during the current session.

Browsers and third-party infrastructure may retain cached files, logs or request information according to their own policies. Their retention periods are controlled by the respective providers.

6. Purpose and Legal Basis

Location processing is used to provide the optional tracking and navigation functions requested by the user.

Where UK data protection law applies to developer-controlled processing, the intended lawful basis is legitimate interests in providing these requested application functions.

Browser location permission is separate from consent under data protection law. You are not required to enable GPS tracking merely to view this policy.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict or object to processing, and request data portability.

UK users may raise data protection concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk .
